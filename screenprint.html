Project overview

Summary

In my New Media and Technology for Social Change class at Engineering and Science University Magnet School, I made screen printed artwork to inspire people to help overcome common societal problems. It was a lengthy process that took multiple months. To start, I looked at other inspirational artwork, then designed a rough draft for my artwork and chose a quote. After that, my classmates and I took pictures of each other, focusing on specific body parts, so that we could use it in our screenprints. After that I set up the picture for my design in Adobe Photoshop as a single color to make it ready to screen print. Then I cleaned up my image and exported it as a PNG so that I could use it in Adobe Illustrator to determine the sizes for each part of my screen. Then I constructed my frame for screen printing and stretched very thin fabric over it. Then I printed the image on vinyl using a Camio. After that I melted the vinyl onto the screen and finally printed my design. I had never screenprinted before or used the majority of these tools despite briefly using Adobe photoshop before.

What I learned

During this project, I faced many new challenges such as unfamiliar tools and guiding myself through the project. This was my first school project that was mostly self-guided, meaning that I was unfamiliar with in-class time management, but luckily as I got further through the project, I was able to set more realistic goals for each day. Secondly, I had to use unfamiliar tools such as advanced Adobe Photoshop settings. This took me a few classes to become familiar with, but every time I learned one, each subsequent one became easier and quicker to learn. Although it was easy and quick to comprehend, this project was the first one that I had to work in multiple apps throughout the project. I was able to take responsibility for my own learning by thoroughly reading through the guide and following instructions to use the new tools. Some things worked the first time, but sometimes there was some trial and error needed to figure out how to use the tools. With practice I was able to use the tools well. To make sure the project was done on time, I used the rubric and guide to set realistic goals for what I needed to complete each day to finish the project on time. I got high quality results by following the guide from the beginning, then finding better and more efficient ways to do things. I also checked over my work at each step to make sure it fit the criteria. I also helped my classmates succeed by helping them to get the photo they felt best suited their project.
