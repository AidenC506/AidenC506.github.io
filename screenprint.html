Aiden's Screenprinting Project

Project overview

Summary

In my New Media and Technology for Social Change class at Engineering and Science University Magnet School, I made screen printed artwork to inspire people to help overcome common societal problems. It was a lengthy process that took multiple months. To start, I looked at other inspirational artwork, then designed a rough draft for my artwork and chose a quote. After that, my classmates and I took pictures of each other, focusing on specific body parts, so that we could use it in our screenprints. After that I set up the picture for my design in Adobe Photoshop as a single color to make it ready to screen print. Then I cleaned up my image and exported it as a PNG so that I could use it in Adobe Illustrator to determine the sizes for each part of my screen. Then I constructed my frame for screen printing and stretched very thin fabric over it. Then I printed the image on vinyl using a Camio. After that I melted the vinyl onto the screen and finally printed my design. I had never screenprinted before or used the majority of these tools despite briefly using Adobe photoshop before.
